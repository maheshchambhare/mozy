QUESTIONS

Quick answers.

Something else? Write to hello@mcrudra.com.

How do steps become screen time?

Mozy counts your steps on your phone. Every 34 steps earns one minute for the apps you've locked. When your minutes run out, those apps lock again.

Which apps can I lock?

Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, games, or any app you pick from your phone's app list.

Does Mozy track my location?

No. Mozy doesn't use GPS, and you don't need an account. Step counting happens on your phone.

Is Mozy free?

The download is free on both stores. On iPhone, the features need Mozy Premium ($4.99 a month or $29.99 a year in the US). On Android, Mozy has in-app purchases.

Which phones does it work on?

iPhone with iOS 16.4 or later, and Android phones with Android 8.0 or later.
