The page explains Mozy. It never shows it.

03 · WHAT'S MISSING

Five gaps, in order of impact.

A · Fix before launch (truth)

  • Free trial. "7-day free trial" is in the FAQ, pricing page, page metadata and JSON-LD. No trial is confirmed on the App Store. Remove it until it's live.
  • Android price. Shown as a $4.99/$29.99 subscription. Google Play lists a free download with in-app purchases ($4.99 to $34.99 per item).
  • Unverified rules. Emergency bypass (3 × 10 min) and the midnight reset are stated as facts. Check them against the current build. "34 steps = 1 minute" is confirmed on iOS only.
  • No Terms page. Subscription apps need one. The live site's /terms and /pricing return 404.
  • Journal clean-up. Remove the ASO packs, render markdown, drop health framing and invented app features, fix "free" CTAs, and verify or cut the first-person "I tried 7 apps for 30 days" result. The content check (npm run test:content) fails today.
  • Old site still live. mozy.mcrudra.com still says "iOS coming soon" with iOS 15 and Android 9 minimums.

B · Story and motion

  • A narrative. One continuous story (night, lock, walk, unlock, world) instead of ten unrelated cards.
  • The lock moment. A full-screen "00:00. Payment declined. Go walk." beat. It's the most memorable thing Mozy does, and it never appears.
  • Scroll-linked motion. Pinned scenes, layered parallax and a counter that counts as you scroll. Today every section does the same fade-up.
  • Worlds and levels. Mozy's most distinctive feature is reduced to a mood picker that teaches nothing about the app.
  • A color journey. Night to sunrise to daylight, so the page itself acts out the promise.

C · Conversion

  • Phone-aware buttons. Lead with the App Store on iPhone and Google Play on Android.
  • A QR code on desktop. Nobody installs Mozy on a laptop. Hand them to their phone.
  • Sticky CTA straight to the store. Today it jumps to #download first: one extra tap.
  • Tracked store links. Apple campaign tokens and Play referrers per button, so you know which section drove installs. The App Store provider token is still missing.
  • Price and requirements next to the buttons. iOS 16.4+, Android 8.0+, and what each platform costs.

D · Trust

  • A founder. One indie developer with a face and a direct email beats an anonymous brand.
  • Real proof only. The fake testimonials are gone (good). Nothing replaced them. Use the Product Hunt page and the App Store privacy label (Data Not Collected).
  • A comparison. Why Mozy instead of a screen-time timer.
  • A fuller footer. Social accounts, MCRUDRAX, Terms, and absolute links (relative /privacy and /terms 404 on subdomains).

E · Code and assets

  • Dead code. 30 of 33 component files are unused (src/components/desktop and the old sections), plus a 2,000-line legacy index.css.
  • Asset hygiene. Duplicates ("home2 copy.avif"), a file with no extension ("heromobileand tablet"), a 2.3 MB hero PNG, and 760 KB PNG screenshots that should be AVIF or WebP.
  • Stale brand docs. BRAND_DESIGN_LANGUAGE.md and the .agents rule still describe the old dark bento grid.
  • Copy bug. "phone.You" on mobile, where a hidden line break swallows the space.
