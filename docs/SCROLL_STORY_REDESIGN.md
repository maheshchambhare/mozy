# Scroll-story landing page — October 10, 2026 (v2)

The landing page is one night-to-day story in six chapters. Every illustration is hand-drawn SVG (no raster art except the app icon, the official store badges and the QR code), so scenes stay sharp, light and animatable.

Moz appears only inside the phone. The timeline pauses while the hero is off screen; with reduced motion CSS shows the locked frame. The phone still turns to face the visitor as the page scrolls; pointer tilt on desktop.

3. **The walk** (`WalkChapter.tsx`, `#walk`; scenery in `walk/sceneryArt.ts` and `walk/SceneryParts.tsx`): pinned 320vh side-scroller, fine-tuned 11 October 2026. The ground reaches the gate exactly when the counter hits 3,400, Moz turns round when you scroll back up, and the step bar steps aside during the walk so two counters never disagree on screen.

## Motion rules
- `useScene(rootRef, setup)` (src/hooks/useScene.ts) runs each chapter's ScrollTrigger setup inside `gsap.matchMedia`, re-running on the 861px breakpoint and the reduced-motion preference, and reverting on unmount. `setup` must be a module-level function.
- Pinned chapters use CSS sticky tracks, never GSAP pinning, so React keeps owning the DOM. Tracks are tall only with `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference`.
- With reduced motion: no movement; scenes show their final state (the walk is already unlocked, the island is a park); How it works switches steps with its buttons. Counters may still change text.
- `useLandingMotion.ts` is page-level only: the step counter bar, `.st-reveal` fades, `[data-depth]` drift and refreshes after fonts load or FAQ answers open.
- Styles live in `src/story.css` (prefix `st-`); the world and final scenes add `src/story-world.css`. All CSS ships in one bundle (`cssCodeSplit: false`).

## Links and attribution
- Every store button uses `storeLink(store, placement)` in `src/data/landing.ts` (MCX campaign format). Placements: NAV, LANDING-HERO, LANDING-STICKY, LANDING-FINAL, PRICING-PAGE, PRICING-PAGE-BOTTOM, GET, GET-QR.
- iPhone visitors get the App Store first, Android visitors Google Play first (`usePlatform`, hydration-safe); the header and the step bar link straight to the visitor's store on phones.
- `/get` sends phones to their store and shows both badges elsewhere. The QR code (`public/assets/story/mozy-qr.svg`, generated with CoreImage and verified by decoding) points to `https://mozy.mcrudra.com/get?src=qr`. `/get` is prerendered with `noindex` and kept out of the sitemap.
- Apple only attributes campaign links once the App Store Connect provider token (`pt=`) is added to `storeLink`.
