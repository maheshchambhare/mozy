# Mozy

> App blocker and step counter: walking earns screen-time minutes for the apps you pick, and those apps lock when the minutes run out.

Mozy is made by mcrudra.com (founder: Mahesh Chambhare). It runs on iOS 16.4+ (iPhone), Android 8.0+.

Pricing: iOS (US): free download; Monthly Premium $4.99, Yearly Premium $29.99; an active subscription is required for app features. Android (US): free with in-app purchases ($4.99-$34.99 per item). The website says free/freemium, which conflicts with iOS.
